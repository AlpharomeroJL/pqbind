# Contributing

**Code contributions are not being accepted yet.** pqbind is offered under two licenses (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)), so a
contributed change can only be merged under a contributor agreement that lets the copyright holder offer it under both. That
agreement is not in place yet.

What is welcome:

- Bug reports and questions as issues, once the repository is public.
- Design feedback, especially on the threat model and on the composition of the hybrid signature. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and
  [THREAT_MODEL.md](THREAT_MODEL.md).
- Security reports: please follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue.
